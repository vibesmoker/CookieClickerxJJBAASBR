JoJo's Cookie Clicker — version 3
=================================

HOW TO PLAY
- Turn the mod on in Cookie Clicker: Options > Mods > "JoJo's Cookie Clicker", then restart.
- The STAND USERS panel sits under the cookie. Each card is the character's All-Star Battle R select portrait.
    i       = info: current stats, cooldown, uptime and chances for that character's level
    GO!     = use the ability (shows the cooldown timer while recharging)
    ▲ cost  = feed cookies to level up (max level 100). Locked/hireable cards show the unlock or hire cost.
- ▼ minimizes the panel; click the golden Stand Arrow at the bottom to bring it back.
- Recruited characters stand under the cookie. Harvest replaces the Cursor building (store icon + the cursors circling the cookie).

THE CREW
- Jotaro (start): Star Platinum barrages the cookie at 15 clicks/sec, no cursor needed. Passive click power.
- Joseph (bake 10,000 cookies): Hermit Purple wraps the cookie for bonus click power.
- Okuyasu (50K): The Hand makes your next click +10,000%. Leveling only trims the 10-minute cooldown.
- Giorno (own 10 buildings), Jonathan (own 25 buildings): passive CpS.
- Jolyne (250K): Stone Free auto-clicks; 5/sec for 5s at Lv1 up to 20/sec for 15s at Lv100, cooldown 10 min to 2 min.
- Avdol (1M): passive CpS, and the milk is on fire.
- Mista (click the cookie 1,000 times): passive click power plus Sex Pistols pass-pass-pass a bullet into the cookie.
- Narancia (5M): Aerosmith strikes a random building you own and boosts its production.
- King Crimson / Diavolo / Doppio (own one of every building, then 1 billion): building purchases may cost nothing
  (0.5% to 50%, upgrades excluded). When it triggers he becomes Doppio: 10% chance every 10s to summon a nail
  (with his phone ring) that cuts click power 5%; click a nail to pull it out. Ten pulled = Diavolo for 5 minutes,
  shortening Mista and Narancia's cooldowns, then back to King Crimson.
- Iggy (own 60 grandmas, then 25M): wanders the screen and digs with The Fool every 30s (20% coffee gum, otherwise
  a chocolate bar). Click him and he runs to the other side. After 5 gum he naps on a random teammate for 10 minutes
  (+passive bonus, shorter cooldown, longer uptime). He won't nap on or walk across King Crimson.
- Dragon's Dream (100M): every 2 minutes, a 50% chance of a giant arrow on the cookie. Click the matching arrow
  (or press that arrow key) within 10 seconds to make your next JoJo upgrade 50% off.

CHANGING THE NUMBERS
Every character has a folder in characters/. Open characters/<name>/stats.js in any text editor.
The STATS list at the top is ordered: 1 stat bonuses, 2 cooldown, 3 uptime, 4 random/chance values.
"Lv1"/"Lv100" pairs set level 1 and level 100; levels in between are filled in automatically
(cooldowns step down every 5 levels; uptime and strength grow every level).
Change a number, save the file, restart Cookie Clicker. Keep the commas and quotes as they are.
If a file has a typo, that character falls back to the built-in default values, so the mod keeps working.
